    Settings

    Failures from one IP inside 10 seconds that mark it as a possible fast brute-force attack.

    Failures inside 10 minutes that count as a slow attack when they are spread out (not a burst).

    Failures inside one hour that count as a slow attack. Catches bots that wait minutes between tries.

    Score weights

    score = short × 10 s failures + medium × 10 min failures + long × 1 h failures + username × (distinct usernames − 1) + target × failures on a username under distributed attack + recent (if a failure happened in the last 10 s).
